# Vision

Status: **Draft, proceeding on creator's standing go-ahead** (DOCS-004, #10) · Plan version 0.2.0 · Decisions: [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)

## Who it's for

Players who loved *Interstellar* for the ache of lost time as much as for the spectacle, and who enjoy choice-driven games where consequences land later and elsewhere. It's free in the browser on desktop and iPhone (C-03, C-10), and a run takes 15–25 minutes, so it has to work for someone who has never played a space game.

## What it should feel like

**"Every minute here costs someone years."** The player should leave a session able to say who paid for their choices, and how much. Spectacle supports that feeling but never replaces it.

## The four pillars (C-07) and how each serves that feeling

| Pillar | In the Miller's planet episode |
| --- | --- |
| Dialogue, relationships, choices | Brand wants Miller's flight recorder; Romilly waits alone and decides whether to hibernate. Who you trust changes what people do when it matters. |
| Piloting and spacecraft operations | An assisted Ranger descent, where you choose a fast fuel-hungry approach or a slow efficient one. Docking back to Endurance is assisted. |
| Exploration and scientific problems | Is Miller's planet viable? The evidence is on the surface (beacon, wreck, waves) and in orbit (Romilly's analysis). Better evidence costs time. |
| Resources, survival, time | Ranger fuel, Endurance supplies, and the shared clock. Time on the surface is spent from Romilly's life and Earth's calendar. |

## Product boundary

- **Full product:** a free, public, single-player campaign across several episodes (Miller, Mann, Gargantua, Earth), with live co-op later (C-04, M5).
- **Episode 1 (this plan):** Miller's planet only (D-01). Cooper and Romilly are playable in M1; Brand and Murph join in M2 (D-02). One scientific problem, one relationship decision, one resource trade-off, and at least two outcomes that differ in game state.
- **Not in episode 1:** the full film plot, the other planets, black-hole flight, the tesseract, voiceover, character customisation, accounts or cloud saves, networking.

## How we'll judge it

1. A first-time player, asked after one run, can name what their surface time cost Romilly. Correct to within a few years counts.
2. Two runs with different choices end in states the player can tell apart without reading any text: who is alive and awake, what is known, how much fuel is left.
3. Nobody reports "I switched to dodge the cost". The rules make that impossible (TIMELINES INV-03), and players should *feel* that it is.
4. iPhone 16e and desktop runs of the same choices end identically (INV-15).
